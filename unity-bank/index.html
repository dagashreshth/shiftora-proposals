Use cases for Unity Bank

Each area is a proposal to assess against current priorities, existing systems and vendor coverage. The relevant business team would define the operating rules and acceptance criteria.

We would work alongside Unity’s Oracle Fusion, Oracle analytics and existing automations.

  1. 01Resolve exceptions in existing automations

    Automated work stops when an input is incomplete, inconsistent or outside the expected pattern.

    Proposed output. Classify the issue, collect the missing evidence and prepare a recovery action for approval and resumption through the existing workflow.

    Illustrative case. A supplier file arrives with a changed layout. The system identifies the affected fields, checks them against the expected record and prepares a corrected input for review.

  2. 02Carry complaints through to resolution

    Resolving a complaint can require evidence and action from several bank teams or an external partner.

    Proposed output. Assemble the case history, identify missing evidence, prepare the response and track the authorised correction or refund to completion.

    Illustrative case. A customer is waiting for a refund. The system brings together the original transaction, partner response and credit status, then prepares the next action and customer explanation.

  3. 03Prepare loans for authorised disbursal

    A sanctioned loan still needs complete evidence that its operational conditions have been met.

    Proposed output. Build a condition-by-condition evidence checklist, flag missing or inconsistent records and route outstanding items to the responsible owner.

    Illustrative case. A case has its approval letter but lacks a valid mandate or evidence for another condition. The system identifies exactly what is missing and prepares the follow-up.

  4. 04Verify product changes before release

    An approved fee, reward or repayment change must produce consistent results across the affected customer journey.

    Proposed output. Translate approved rules into reviewable test scenarios, compare expected and actual results, and produce a traceable defect report.

    Illustrative case. A reward rule changes how a reversed purchase is treated. Test cases cover the purchase, refund and reward reversal, including boundary dates and amounts.

  5. 05Complete invoice exception work

    An invoice can remain blocked while a branch, supplier and approver resolve missing acceptance or disputed charges.

    Proposed output. Bring together the invoice, contract and acceptance evidence; identify the blocker and prepare the follow-up needed to return the case to the payment process.

    Illustrative case. An invoice bills the full service period, but only part has been accepted. The system assembles the supporting records and routes the discrepancy for an authorised decision.

  6. 06Complete funded deposit applications

    An application can involve separate payment, verification and deposit-activation states across the bank and a partner.

    Proposed output. Trace the application across those states, identify pending work and route the authorised completion or refund with a clear customer update.

    Illustrative case. The customer has paid but verification is incomplete. The system identifies the current blocker, prepares the required action and tracks the resulting activation or refund.

  7. 07Finish loan closure and release tasks

    Repayment is followed by several closure tasks, each with its own evidence, owner and completion status.

    Proposed output. Track approved adjustments, closure documents, bureau updates and security-release tasks; prepare follow-ups and escalate outstanding items.

    Illustrative case. Repayment is confirmed, but a closure document or release task remains pending. The system identifies the owner and the evidence needed to finish it.

Unity retains all decisions, approvals and customer communications.
