02

Why this is revenue, not operating cost

Process automation is usually sold on cost per file. That is the wrong number here, because your delivery sits offshore and squeezing it moves almost nothing. The exposure is on the other side of the ledger. Everything below is published regulation or arithmetic on your published headcount.

  • A lapsed permit is a stop, not a delay. It is illegal to work in the UAE without a valid MoHRE work permit, and the Emirates ID runs on the same validity window. The associate does not slow down. They come off site.
  • As employer of record, the exposure lands on you. The sponsorship sits with TASC, not the client, and so does the penalty: AED 200 per month past the sixty-day grace period, and up to AED 50,000 per violation for employing someone without a valid permit.
  • The volume is structural, not occasional. Private-sector labour cards run two years. Across 7,500 associates that is roughly 310 renewal events every month, before Saudi Iqamas, medicals and passport expiries are counted.
  • Nobody is failing at this. It is held together by capable people, spreadsheets and reminders, and that works until it is one file among three hundred in a month. The cost of the one that slips is a client asking why their person is not on site.

03

Where we would start

One client account, one emirate, read-only, running quietly beside the live process. Small enough that judging us costs TASC very little, and specific enough that the result is not arguable afterwards.

Week 1

Shadow

The chain reads what already exists for one account. No integration, no change to how anyone works. The output is a baseline you do not have today: eligibility per associate, per day.

Week 2

Measure

The two empty rows above get filled with real numbers: current lapse rate, and billable days lost to it. This is the point at which you decide whether the rest is worth anything.

Week 3

Surface

Forward projection live. What lapses inside ninety days, who has to act, and by when, delivered where your team already works rather than in a new screen.

After

Only if earned

Widening to a second account or into Saudi happens because the week-two number justified it. If it did not, we say so, and it stops there.

How you hold us to it

  • One number. Billable days recovered, agreed in writing before we start, evaluated on a date fixed in advance.
  • Fixed fee. Scoped after we have seen one account. All costs inside it, so the pilot cannot quietly grow.
  • Missed is our problem. If the agreed number is not met, that is explicit and the commercial consequence sits with us.
  • Your code, your tenant. Source code and an isolated environment, both yours. Continuity is a design requirement, not a promise.

What we will not do

  • Not price extraction as intelligence. Where a step is a commodity we will tell you and price it as one.
  • Not replace Salesforce or Agentforce. You have made that investment and it is working. We build behind it or not at all.
  • Not decide about a person. The system reads, tracks and warns. Whether someone is deployed, renewed or replaced stays your team's call.
  • Not name you. Other clients in this region are unnamed in our material for exactly the reason you would be.
