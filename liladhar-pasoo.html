What this means in practice

  • The doc pack is read once, at arrival. The checklist, the filing fields and the query answers all derive from that single reading.
  • The checklist builds itself. Per commodity, scheme and port, from requirements held as versioned configuration rather than remembered.
  • Repeat goods are recalled, not re-judged. Your own precedent is proposed with a confidence score, and your seniors spend judgement where it is genuinely new.
  • The clocks are watched. Arrivals, holds and demurrage windows tracked per file, so a delay is seen while it is still cheap.

Why Liladhar Pasoo, and why now

  • The file is the product. Clients hand you their compliance. The whole service is delivered as a correctly assembled, correctly declared file.
  • The rules are published. The tariff, the notifications and the agency requirements are issued and versioned by the government itself. Configuration before judgement.
  • Nothing is replaced. No change with customs, no change for clients, no migration. This reads the paper, not the port.
  • Your history is the asset. A century of clearances is classification precedent nobody else in the market has. It simply is not searchable yet.

03

What we see at Liladhar Pasoo

Four observations from public sources, each with the business consequence attached.

Observation 01 · the judgement

Your product is knowing where things fit.

Classification decides the duty. The notification decides what relief applies. A century of getting both right is why clients hand you their files instead of filing themselves.

But most files repeat goods you have cleared many times before. Today the repeat work and the judgement work consume the same senior hours, because nothing distinguishes them until a person has read the file.

Observation 02 · the file

Every clearance starts as somebody’s doc pack.

Invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificates, licences, in whatever format the client keeps them. The same facts are read by several people and typed once more at the end, into the filing.

Errors surface as customs queries, after filing, when they cost demurrage and detention on cargo that is already at the port.

Observation 03 · the rulebooks

The requirements change under your feet.

Notifications amend duty overnight. Trade agreements rewrite origin rules. Agency requirements differ by commodity and change without ceremony. Keeping up is the job, and your people do keep up.

They keep up in their heads. The rules are published and versioned by the government itself, which makes memory the most expensive possible place to store them.

Observation 04 · the clock

Your client is measured in days, so you are.

A query, a hold or a missing paper costs the client demurrage by the day, and the client remembers the days at renewal, not the difficulty.

The slowest step is rarely customs. It is assembling answers from documents you already hold, by hand, while the clock runs on somebody else’s cargo.

Why this matters commercially

None of this is a failure. A hundred years in customs is built on exactly this care, applied by people who know the tariff better than the market does.

The opportunity is that most of a clearance is reading documents against published requirements and recalling what you have cleared before. Both are system work now. Your seniors’ judgement stays for the files that are genuinely new, which is where it was always meant to be spent.

Your customs relationships, your client relationships and your licence stay untouched.

How we work

  • One agreed number. Fixed in writing before we start, measured on a date set in advance.
  • Weekly reporting. The same format every week, good news or bad.
  • People decide. The system reads, checks and proposes. Your officers classify, confirm and file.
  • Your environment. Data stays in your systems and jurisdiction. We integrate rather than extract.
  • We stay. Monitoring, updates, retraining and support for the life of the system.

What we will not do

  • Change how clients reach you. The same branches, the same people, the same relationships. This sits behind them.
  • Replace judgement. A proposed heading is a starting point with its precedent attached. What is declared to customs stays your seniors’ call.
  • File anything automatically. No bill of entry, no shipping bill and no query reply reaches customs without a person approving it.
  • Expose you to other clients. We work with logistics businesses and carry nothing between them, in either direction. Isolation is audited, not assumed.
  • Create dependency. Your team is trained to run it without us.
